<!-- Best Practices -->
<div style="background: linear-gradient(135deg, #4A3429 0%, #2c2c2c 100%); color: white; padding: 8px 12px; border-radius: 10px; margin-top: 6px; box-shadow: 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.1);">
    <h3 style="margin: 0 0 3px 0; font-size: 1.45em; font-weight: 600;">Best Practices for Success Criteria</h3>
    <ul style="margin: 3px 0 0 0; padding-left: 20px; line-height: 1.5; opacity: 0.95; font-size: 1.05em;">
        <li style="margin: 0;">Be specific and measurable - avoid vague terms like "better" or "improved"</li>
        <li style="margin: 0;">Ensure criteria are achievable given your resources and timeline</li>
        <li style="margin: 0;">Include both quantitative and qualitative measures</li>
        <li style="margin: 0;">Consider different stakeholder perspectives on success</li>
        <li style="margin: 0;">Plan how you'll actually collect the measurement data</li>
    </ul>
